Tumhare liye traffic pareshani hai par usi se yeh bande ne 2 lakh plus monthly revenue kar raha hai.
Sab log shaam ko kaam se aate the, to Mumbai mein 20 se 45 minute tak traffic jam mein phans jaate the.
Jahaan usko bhookh lagi thi, paani chahiye tha, aur gate se bahar bhi nahi nikal sakta gaadi ke.
To isi problem ko is bande ne solve kiya.
Yahin se Traffic Vada Pav ka start hua.
To isne smartly package kiya, usmein ek Vada Pav, ek water bottle aur ek tissue paper.
Sab ko ek acche se packaging karke 20 rupya mein bechta tha.
Shuru mein to koi nahi khareedta tha, kyunki wahi hygiene wagairah sabka kami ke tarah.
Over the time iska mehnat ne rang laya, aur aaj 2 lakh plus ka monthly revenue kar raha hai.
Dekho India mein talent ki kami nahi hai, kami hai to execution ki.
