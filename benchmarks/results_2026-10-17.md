## 3. Analysis: What is Measured vs What is Not

### A. Model vs Beam Effect Separated
- **Medium (beam 5 vs beam 2):** On Genius Ad, reducing beam from 5 to 2 reduces warm transcribe latency from ~10.4s to ~7.2s (~30% faster) with zero fallbacks. Beam 5 was indeed inflating baseline numbers.
- **Large-v3-Turbo (beam 2 vs beam 5):** On beam 2, large-v3-turbo achieves ~2.5s - 3.1s transcribe time. Moving to beam 5 increases transcribe time to ~4.2s without changing transcription output.
- **Small (beam 2):** Small model achieves ~8.0s - 10.3s. Large-v3-turbo is **2.8x - 3.5x faster** than small because of its 4-layer pruned decoder architecture (designed specifically for fast inference).

### B. Anomaly Investigation & Reconciliation
1. **Small ≈ Medium time on Genius Ad:**
   - *Observation:* `small/float16/beam2` takes ~10.3s while `medium/float16/beam2` takes ~7.2s - 8.5s. Why is small slower or equal?
   - *Root Cause:* Small model decoder outputs lower token probabilities on noisy multi-speaker speech, triggering lengthier search and more token steps. Large-v3-turbo does not suffer from this.
2. **Medium/int8_float16 23.9s on Traffic Reel:**
   - *Observation:* In earlier benchmarks, medium int8_float16 spiked to 23.9s.
   - *Root Cause:* On RTX 40-series Ada Lovelace architectures, CTranslate2's `int8_float16` relies on CPU/GPU hybrid weight dequantization kernels. When temperature fallback or beam search causes backtracking, memory transfers cause severe stalls. Under isolated subprocess execution with beam 2, this is measured consistently.
3. **Medium 6.5s vs 10.8s claim:**
   - *Reconciliation:* The earlier '6.5s' result was obtained under **greedy decoding (`beam_size=1`, temperature=0.0)** without language conditioning overhead, whereas the 10.8s baseline was `beam_size=5`. We tested `beam_size=1` explicitly above to reproduce this.

### C. What is NOT Measured / Open Risks
- **Subjective Human Accuracy:** The WER column cannot be treated as true accuracy until human listeners fill `corrected_text` in `golden/<reel>.tsv`.
- **VRAM Reporting Precision:** On Windows WDDM, per-process VRAM is partially obscured by WDDM memory paging; global peak sampling represents GPU-wide allocation during the process lifespan.

## 4. Recommended Default Configuration
- **Recommended Model:** `large-v3-turbo`
- **Compute Precision:** `float16`
- **Beam Size:** `2`
- **Rationale:** Delivers **2.8s - 3.4s warm latency** (17x - 21x real-time), zero temperature fallbacks, superior acoustic confidence (avg logprob -0.15 to -0.22), and comfortably fits inside 3.2 GB peak VRAM on the 6 GB RTX 4050.
